# Sales Data Analysis Dashboard - Excel Project 2

![Sales Analysis Dashboard](/Images/1-Sales%20Data%20Analysis%20Dashboard.PNG)

## Introduction
Understanding sales performance, revenue distribution, and customer purchasing patterns is critical for business growth and operational efficiency. This project delivers an interactive **Sales Data Analysis Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Excel**, designed to aggregate raw transaction data into executive-level visual insights.

By analyzing key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total sales, profit margins, regional performance, and product category trends, this dashboard equips business leaders and sales managers with an intuitive decision-support tool to identify revenue drivers and optimize strategy.

---

## Skills Showcased
- **Data Preparation & ETL:** Cleaned, structured, and transformed raw transactional datasets in Excel by standardizing date formats, removing duplicates, and handling missing attributes.
- **Advanced Formulas & Functions:** Leveraged Excel functions (`SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `XLOOKUP`, `IF`) to build dynamic aggregated summary tables.
- **Data Modeling with Pivot Tables:** Constructed multiple robust **Pivot Tables** to summarize sales performance across regions, product lines, sales channels, and time periods.
- **Calculated Fields & Metrics:** Formulated core business KPIs including `Total Revenue`, `Total Profit`, `Profit Margin %`, `Total Units Sold`, and `Average Order Value (AOV)`.
- **Data Visualization:** Built visual components including **Combo Charts**, **Stacked Bar/Column Charts**, **Donut Charts**, and **Trend Line Charts** to communicate complex sales trends clearly.
- **Interactive Dashboard Design:** Implemented dynamic **Slicers** and filter controls for seamless regional, category, and temporal data exploration.

---

## Key Business Insights
- **Revenue & Profitability:** High revenue totals are driven primarily by top-performing product lines, with distinct categories maintaining higher profit margins.
- **Geographic Performance:** Regional sales distributions highlight top revenue-generating territories while pinpointing underperforming markets for strategic intervention.
- **Product Category Dominance:** A concentrated subset of product categories accounts for the majority of overall sales volume, indicating opportunities for inventory and campaign optimization.
- **Temporal & Monthly Trends:** Multi-month sales tracking reveals seasonal peaks in demand, helping forecast inventory requirements and revenue targets.

## Dashboard Overview

### Main Dashboard Page: Executive Sales Overview

![Sales Performance Dashboard](/Images/1-Sales%20Data%20Analysis%20Dashboard.PNG)

The main interactive dashboard serves as a centralized hub for monitoring end-to-end sales performance:
- **KPI Summary Cards:** Highlights top-line business indicators including *Total Sales*, *Total Profit*, *Profit Margin %*, *Total Orders*, and *Average Order Value*.
- **Sales & Profit Trend Analysis:** A dynamic trend chart depicting monthly revenue fluctuations and profitability over time.
- **Category & Product Breakdown:** Visual representations comparing sales volume and revenue across major product categories and sub-categories.
- **Regional & Channel Distribution:** Displays market share and sales contribution across different geographic regions and sales channels.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Enables real-time dynamic filtering by *Region*, *Product Category*, *Sales Channel*, and *Date Range*.

### Summary & Backend Analysis Sheets

In addition to the main visual dashboard, the workbook incorporates dedicated summary and pivot sheets (`Analysis`) that form the analytical core:
- **Data Model Foundation:** Converts raw transaction logs into structured Excel tables optimized for rapid querying.
- **Backend Summary Tables:** Houses cross-tabulations and metric calculations that feed directly into the visual dashboard charts.

---

## Project Architecture & Methodology
1. **Raw Data Ingestion & Cleaning:** Standardized column types, handled blank values, and created derived attributes (e.g., Year, Month, Profit Margin).
2. **Pivot Table Aggregation:** Synthesized transaction records into high-level summaries across key dimensions (Geography, Category, Time).
3. **KPI & Formula Modeling:** Applied custom Excel calculations to derive accurate margin ratios and performance benchmarks.
4. **Front-End Dashboard Layout:** Designed a clean visual UI incorporating metric cards, standardized color schemes, dynamic slicers, and chart elements.

This Excel project demonstrates how raw sales data can be converted into an impactful, interactive reporting solution.
